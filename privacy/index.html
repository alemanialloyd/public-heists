Ghost Heist Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Ghost Heist game (app ID com.bof.ghostheist, on iOS and Android) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. "We" means the developer of Ghost Heist.

In short:

  • Ghost Heist has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • Your progress, stars, coins and outfits are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID and other device data to show and measure ads.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (levels completed and stars), coins, the outfits you own and wear, and your sound and music settings in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Downloading new heists

When the game opens, it downloads a file of heist levels from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) so new levels can arrive without an app update. Like any web request, this shares your IP address and basic device information with GitHub, which handles it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. No information about you or your game is included in the request, and we do not receive any logs from it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ghost Heist is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: rewarded ads that you choose to watch (for example, to double your coins), and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show, limit, measure and prevent fraud in ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google and its advertising partners:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS when you allow it, or the Android advertising ID);
  • IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location;
  • device and app information such as device model, operating system version, language, and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostic data such as crashes of the ad SDK.

We do not see this data directly; we only get combined reports from AdMob, such as how many ads were shown. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, the game shows Google's consent message, where you can accept or decline personalised ads and the related data use. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change your ad consent choices at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • Tracking on iOS: Apple's "Allow tracking" prompt asks before the advertising ID (IDFA) is used. If you tap "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared. You can change this any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).
  • US state privacy rights: where Google supports restricted data processing for your state, ads are served accordingly.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use any analytics service of our own, and we do not create accounts or profiles.
  • The game has no chat, no social features, and no in-app purchases.

Children

Ghost Heist is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies; you can reset your advertising ID as described above, and you can manage Google data at myaccount.google.com.

Security

The level download uses HTTPS. Since we do not receive or store personal information, no personal information is held on our servers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or your data, open an issue at github.com/alemanialloyd/public-heists/issues.
